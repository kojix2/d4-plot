## Explore genomic signals on your desktop

D4 Plot is a cross-platform desktop viewer for D4 genomic signal files. It is
designed for biologists and medical researchers who want to inspect coverage,
compare samples, check genomic regions, and export results without writing
code. It can also create coverage D4 files from BAM or CRAM.

Start with the [English five-minute workflow](books/guide_en/quick-start.html)
or [日本語の「5分で見る」](books/guide_ja/quick-start.html). You do not need the
command line for normal use.
